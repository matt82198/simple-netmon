# Code of Conduct — vigil

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers of vigil pledge to make participation in this project a
respectful, constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience
level. We are committed to technical collaboration that is professional, good-faith, and
focused on the work.

## Our Standards

**Encouraged behavior:**

- Communicating clearly and respectfully in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Giving and receiving technical feedback in good faith
- Acknowledging mistakes, correcting course, and focusing on outcomes over credit

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment directed at any contributor
- Deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption of project discussions
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that does not meet these standards. Enforcement decisions are at maintainer discretion
and may be made without prior notice.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face
consequences as determined by other project leads.

## Reporting

To report a violation, open a GitHub issue (for non-sensitive reports) or use GitHub's
Private Vulnerability Reporting for sensitive matters.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
